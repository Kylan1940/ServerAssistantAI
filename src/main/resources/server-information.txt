=============================
COMMANDS
=============================
General:
/spawn - Teleport to the server spawn.
/rtp - Teleport to a random location.
/help - Show available commands.

Economy:
/shop - Open the server shop.
/sell - Sell items.
/balance - Check your balance.
/pay <player> <amount> - Send money to another player.

Land:
/land buy - Buy the land you are standing in.
/land claim - Claim land.
/land trust <player> - Allow another player to build in your land.
/land untrust <player> - Remove a trusted player.

=============================
LAND
=============================
Players can protect their builds using the land protection system.

To buy a land, stand inside the desired area and use:
/land buy

Only the land owner and trusted players can build inside protected land.

=============================
